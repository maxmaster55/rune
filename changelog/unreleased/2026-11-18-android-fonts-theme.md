- Android: text in scripts the app's fonts lack (Arabic, Devanagari, CJK, emoji) draws in the system's fonts, found with the NDK's font matcher (API 29) without walking `/system/fonts`. A window is light or dark as the phone is set, and follows a change; winit sends `ScaleFactorChanged` only when the density changes, not on every configuration change. `examples/android-hello` builds and links in CI (`.github/workflows/android.yml`).
