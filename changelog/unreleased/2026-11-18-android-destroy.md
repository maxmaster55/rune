- Android: an app no longer hangs when its activity is destroyed. The vendored winit now ends the event loop on `onDestroy`, so `android_main` returns and `NativeActivity.onDestroy`, which waits for it, can finish. Back now reaches the activity, so it closes the app as on any Android app; before, winit kept it and the app could not be left with Back. Switching the phone between light and dark while the app is open now switches the app: the night mode is read from the activity's `Resources`, because the configuration copied at launch keeps its launch value. `examples/android-demo` is a tap counter with a dark-mode switch, checked on an Android 13 x86_64 emulator.
